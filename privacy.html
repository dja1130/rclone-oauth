Privacy policy

homeserveransell rclone is used by one person, the owner of this GitHub account, to access their own Google Drive.

  • What it accesses: files and folders in the owner's own Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, only to upload encrypted backups and to read folders the owner chose.
  • Where data goes: between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's home server. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or service.
  • What is stored: the OAuth token is kept only on the owner's home server. No analytics, tracking, advertising or data sales of any kind.
  • Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.

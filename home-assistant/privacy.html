Privacy policy

homeserveransell Home Assistant is used by one person, the owner of this GitHub account, to access their own Google account.

What it accesses: the owner's own Google Calendar events and Google Tasks lists, through the Google Calendar API and the Google Tasks API, to display them on the owner's home screens and, when the owner asks, to add or complete their own events and tasks.

Where data goes: between the owner's Google account and the owner's home server, and from there to screens inside the owner's home. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or service.

What is stored: the OAuth token and a short-lived copy of upcoming events and tasks are kept only on the owner's home server. No analytics, tracking, advertising or data sales of any kind.

Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
